# Research directions

What we publish here tracks Greybound’s actual offer:

1. **Wallet and signing applications** — keys, PSBT pipelines, approvals, releases  
2. **Metaprotocols and indexers** — Ordinals / BRC-20 / Runes-class trust at sign time  
3. **Protocol implementations** — correctness and fund-loss risk in clients and bridges  
4. **Production backends and operations** — authz, multi-tenant isolation, webhooks, nodes, deploy trust  
5. **Remediation patterns** — closing serious issues in the real repository  

Not primary for this repo: Lightning/swap specialist deep dives; hardware silicon reverse engineering without a completed engagement behind it.
